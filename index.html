Workshop Outline: The Power Equation

A working draft for Rich's table-format workshop, built from the case studies already pulled from his own content. Ties directly into November's “power equation” theme and the Dr. Scott Peck case study already planned for that session.

Format
Interactive, table-based — not a presentation
Length
60–75 minutes
Audience
Hero Club — CEOs, founders, investors
Theme link
Power equation · invisible assets, real leverage
Agenda

Suggested flow

0–8 min

Open with the multiplier

Rich opens with the data point already in his own deck: in 1975 the biggest companies were 5x tangible-to-intangible; today it's the reverse, roughly 1:5.5. IP has become 30x more valuable in four decades. Every person in the room already owns more of this than they think.

8–28 min
Table Exercise 1

Good IP, Poor IP, No IP

Each table gets ONE real case (not hypothetical) and has 12 minutes to answer: what made the IP good or poor, and what would you have done differently?

  • Table A — Wayland Furniture: a $100M furniture company's stalled patent finally issues, they build a 3-patent “fortress” around it, and receive a $300M acquisition offer.
  • Table B — Stephen Key vs. P&G: P&G rejects his label patent outright (“we're not going to pay you one penny”) — he still licenses it elsewhere and sells 500M+ units over 20 years.
  • Table C — The $2M EBITDA calculator: same business, same $60K IP spend, three outcomes — $6M, $6.6M, or $12M at exit, depending only on IP quality.
28–38 min

Report back

One person per table, 90 seconds each. Rich connects the three cases back to one line: the gap between good and poor IP is strategy, not spend.

38–58 min
Table Exercise 2

Find Your Own Rembrandt

Using the 4 categories already in Rich's own deck — Products (Patent), Branding (Trademark), Content (Copyright), and Know-how — each attendee lists one asset in their own business they've never formally protected. Tables discuss: is it protectable, is it marketable, and where's the overlap? That overlap is a strategic IP opportunity, not just a legal one.

58–70 min

Close on the exit lens

Rich's own closing line works as-is: “Look toward the end, look toward the exit, look toward the acquisition — that's when you'll get the real ROI.” Offer: a free 15-minute 1:1 for anyone who wants to talk through what they found at their table.
